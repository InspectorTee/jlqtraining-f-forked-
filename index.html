About Us

JLQ Enterprise and Trading (Pty) Ltd, founded in 2021 in Nigel, provides quality computer skills training to communities, schools, and tertiary institutions.

Our mission is to shape the future by equipping people with practical computer knowledge. Our vision is to empower communities across South Africa through modern, relevant, and accessible training.
